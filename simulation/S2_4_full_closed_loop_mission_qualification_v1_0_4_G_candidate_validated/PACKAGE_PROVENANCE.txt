v1.0.3 change
-------------
No runtime/autonomy change from v1.0.2.
The qualification wrapper no longer defines critical-run mission completion using the entire nominal summary.pass. Critical runs now separately require:
- literal mission COMPLETE outcome;
- every mission-manager composite gate except only nominal explorationPass;
- all existing G hard-safety, truth-isolation, fault-response and bounded-retry gates.
No-fault seeds continue to require the complete nominal summary.pass, and E+F is fully rerun before G.

Rationale
---------
ACTIVE_GOAL_REQUIRES_SCAN requires expectedMinExplorationExecutions=1 for nominal E qualification. After a fault revokes the selected viewpoint authority, current-map replanning may validly expose a direct route to the mission goal. Forcing the revoked viewpoint to be executed solely to satisfy the nominal E metric is neither a mission-completion requirement nor a safety requirement. E's exploration contract is already independently qualified before the fault matrix.

v1.0.4 change
-------------
The exploration-viewing yaw is no longer injected into the geometric controller as an angle step. It is stored as a target and shortest-path slew-limited during TRACK_OUTBOUND using the already-qualified inherited S2.3 mapScanYawRate_radps = 35 deg/s. On F/perception/reference-guard authority revocation, the stale viewing-yaw target is discarded and the controller reference is reset to the current estimated yaw during brake/replan. The 2.0 deg estimator threshold, geometric-controller gains, ESKF, plant, planner, E geometry/policy and frozen S2.3 are unchanged.

Local validation includes a source-faithful yaw-axis first-principles backtest using the inherited Jz, attitude/rate gains, angular drag, moment saturation and dt. A 90 deg angle step produces ~199.8 deg/s peak yaw rate, consistent with the measured ~204.9 deg/s class; the 35 deg/s slew produces ~45.2 deg/s peak in the same yaw-axis model. This is mechanism evidence, not a substitute for the coupled MATLAB ESKF run.
